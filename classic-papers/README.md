# 经典论文阅读入口

本目录保存论文索引和原创阅读笔记，不上传受版权限制的论文 PDF。

第一次学习阶段先认识以下四篇：

1. Jackson (2014), “Networks in the Understanding of Economic Behaviors.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.28.4.3)
2. Jackson and Wolinsky (1996), “A Strategic Model of Social and Economic Networks.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/jeth.1996.0108)
3. Bala and Goyal (2000), “A Noncooperative Model of Network Formation.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-0262.00155)
4. Ballester, Calvó-Armengol, and Zenou (2006), “Who’s Who in Networks. Wanted: The Key Player.” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2006.00709.x)

推荐顺序是先读第 1 篇了解研究地图，再在后续单元中分别进入网络形成和关键节点模型。这里暂时不要求一次读完。
